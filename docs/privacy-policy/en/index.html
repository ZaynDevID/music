Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Last updated 2 November 2026

In short: Music is an offline audio player. The app has no account, ads, analytics, or internet permission. Your music files and library data stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

1. Who operates this app

Music (package com.zayndev.music) is developed and published by ZaynDev. Privacy questions can be sent using the Developer email button on the app's official Google Play listing.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Audio files on your device. With your permission, the app reads audio files from device storage or from a folder you choose so it can display and play them.
  • App settings. Theme, equalizer, sleep timer, and display preferences are stored locally.
  • Playlists and folder imports. Lists you create or import are stored locally in the app's storage.
  • Crash logs. If the app encounters an error, technical details (including device model and Android version) may be written to the app folder and/or Downloads/Musik. These logs are not sent automatically and remain on your device.

The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or browsing history.

3. Permissions used

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+) and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): read audio files.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show playback controls in notifications.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: keep music playing in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK and MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: keep playback stable and manage audio.

Permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked at any time in Android settings.

4. Collection and sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app uses no advertising SDK, analytics, tracking, or third-party services that transmit data. No data is sent to our servers because the app has no internet connection.

5. Storage and deletion

Library data, playlists, and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Original audio files are not modified or deleted by the app. Crash logs in Downloads/Musik can be deleted with a file manager.

6. Security

Because data is not sent off the device, protection depends mainly on your device security. We recommend keeping a screen lock and backing up important music files separately.

7. Children

The app is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It only plays audio files already on the device or selected by the user.

8. Links to other sites

The Privacy Policy menu opens this page in a browser. If you follow another link, that site's privacy policy applies. The app does not send data to the browser.

9. Changes

If data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The latest version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or requests, use the Developer email button on the Google Play listing for Music. We will respond through the channel available on that listing.
